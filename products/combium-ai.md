# Combium AI

> **The intelligent layer that makes your projects grow.**  
> **AI orchestration at the core of your projects.**

## Overview

**Combium AI** is a reusable AI project orchestration platform being developed within the **DaliDev-IA** ecosystem.

Its purpose is to coordinate AI models, tools, workflows and human approvals around real projects while keeping execution controlled, traceable and reusable.

Combium AI is designed as an independent platform rather than as a feature tied to a single application. The same orchestration layer can support different products, teams and project types.

**Public product · Private implementation**

The product vision, capabilities, progress and demonstrations are intended to be shared publicly. Proprietary workflows, internal implementation details, credentials, business logic and sensitive orchestration rules remain private.

---

## Why the name Combium?

The name is inspired by the **cambium**, the living growth layer of a tree.

That metaphor reflects the role of the platform: a central layer that connects resources, coordinates growth and supports the development of multiple branches — each branch representing a different project, product or workflow.

In the DaliDev-IA ecosystem, Combium AI is intended to become that shared intelligent layer.

---

## What Combium AI is designed to do

- orchestrate AI-assisted tasks across multiple projects;
- connect different AI models and external tools;
- prepare actions before execution;
- request human approval when decisions require control;
- separate reading, preparation, authorization and execution;
- maintain traceability through task states and logs;
- apply limits around cost, duration and iteration;
- support model selection according to quality, cost and task criticality;
- provide reusable workflows rather than rebuilding automation for every project;
- evolve toward a methodology-aware project orchestration engine.

---

## Human-in-the-loop by design

Automation should not mean losing control.

Combium AI is built around the idea that AI can analyze context, prepare work and coordinate tools, while important actions can remain subject to explicit human validation.

This makes it possible to automate aggressively where appropriate while preserving oversight for sensitive or consequential actions.

---

## Multi-project architecture

Combium AI is not limited to one product.

The orchestration layer is intended to support:

- AI-powered applications;
- software-development projects;
- operational workflows;
- research and analysis tasks;
- content and educational workflows;
- future DaliDev-IA products;
- external projects that need structured AI orchestration.

This is one of the central design principles of the platform: **build the orchestration capability once, then reuse it across projects.**

---

## Current direction

The platform is evolving from a working n8n-based orchestration workflow toward a more complete reusable system.

Current areas of development include:

- task lifecycle management;
- human approval flows;
- controlled project access;
- auditability and execution logs;
- reusable multi-project configuration;
- AI model orchestration;
- cost and usage controls;
- methodology-driven project assistance;
- integration with external services and development tools.

The underlying technology may evolve over time. **Combium AI is the product; n8n is currently one of the technologies used to build it.**

---

## Relationship with DaliDev-IA products

Combium AI is intended to operate as an independent orchestration platform while also being reusable inside other products.

Examples include:

**Coach Master AI**  
Combium AI can provide structured orchestration capabilities for project planning, methodology selection, controlled tool use and multi-step execution.

**Poker Coach AI**  
The same orchestration principles can support controlled analysis pipelines and future product workflows without exposing proprietary implementation.

Future products can reuse the same orchestration layer rather than rebuilding their AI infrastructure from scratch.

---

## Product principles

**Reusable by design** — one orchestration layer for many projects.  
**Human-controlled** — important actions can require explicit validation.  
**Traceable** — tasks and decisions should be understandable after execution.  
**Cost-aware** — AI resources should be selected according to actual needs.  
**Model-agnostic** — the product should not depend permanently on one AI provider.  
**Tool-agnostic** — implementation technologies can evolve without redefining the product.  
**Methodology-aware** — project workflows can adapt to structured methods instead of relying only on free-form prompting.
